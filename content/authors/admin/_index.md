I am a postdoctoral research scientist in the [School of Teaching and Learning](https://education.ufl.edu/school-teaching-learning/) at the University of Florida.

My research examines the cognitive processes that shape numerical understanding — especially **how learners build on what they already know** to accommodate new mathematical concepts. I study how these **skills develop across age groups** and how **AI-mediated learning environments** shape that development, with the goal of building educational technology that reflects how people actually think and learn.

I earned my Ph.D. in the [Department of Psychology](https://psych.wisc.edu/) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I was a member of the [Cognitive Development and Communication Lab](https://alibali.psych.wisc.edu/).
